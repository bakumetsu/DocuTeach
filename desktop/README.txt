Start DocuTeach by double-clicking DocuTeach.exe. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves this PC.
Your lessons are saved as plain files in %APPDATA%\DocuTeach\data (keep this folder to keep your lessons).
DocuTeach.exe is not code-signed, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway". Check the zip with: certutil -hashfile <zip> SHA256 and compare it with SHA256SUMS.txt.
