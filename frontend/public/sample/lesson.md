# Present Perfect: Unfinished Time

The **present perfect** connects past actions to the present. We use it with *since* and *for* to talk about unfinished time.

## 1. Rule

- Subject + **has/have** + past participle
- *since* → a point in time; *for* → a duration
- Not used with finished time words (*yesterday, last year*)

::: teacher {tag="CCQ"}
Has she lived here since 2019? → Yes, she has.
:::

## 2. Practice

1. She {{has lived | 's lived}} (live) in Hanoi for six years.
2. They {{have known | know}} (know) each other since primary school.
3. He {{has worked | works}} (work) at the factory for three months.

She ______ in Hanoi since 2019.
- [ ] lives
- [x] has lived
- [ ] is living

::: teacher {tag="Distractors"}
*lives*: present simple cannot take *since*.
:::
